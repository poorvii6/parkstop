1. What ParkStop does

ParkStop is a marketplace that connects people who have parking spaces to offer (“Spotters”) with people looking to book parking (“Finders”). We facilitate discovery, booking, and payment for parking spots. We are an intermediary and do not own or operate the parking spaces listed on the Platform.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information — your name, email address, phone number, and role (Finder or Spotter), collected when you register. Authentication is handled through Google Firebase.
  • Location data — your device location, used to show nearby parking spots, provide directions, and let Spotters list a spot’s location. You can control location access through your device settings.
  • Booking and transaction data — details of the spots you book or list, dates, times, amounts, and booking history.
  • Payment information — payments are processed by our payment partner, Razorpay. We do not store your full card numbers, UPI PINs, or bank credentials on our servers.
  • Spotter payout details — if you are a Spotter, we collect the PAN and bank account details needed to verify you and pay out your earnings (this verification, known as KYC, is carried out by Razorpay).
  • Device and usage data — device type, app version, and basic diagnostic information used to keep the app working and secure.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and provide the Platform’s features.
  • To match Finders with Spotters and enable bookings.
  • To process payments from Finders and payouts to Spotters.
  • To provide customer support and respond to your requests.
  • To keep the Platform safe, prevent fraud, and enforce our Terms of Service.
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

4. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to run the Platform:

  • Between users — limited booking information is shared between a Finder and Spotter to complete a booking (for example, the spot location and booking time).
  • Razorpay — to process payments, split platform fees, and pay out Spotters.
  • Service providers — such as Google Firebase (authentication, notifications), Google Maps (location and mapping), and our hosting provider, who process data on our behalf.
  • Legal reasons — where required by law, regulation, or valid legal process, or to protect the rights and safety of users and the public.

5. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the service, comply with legal, tax, and accounting obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

6. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, and payment data is handled by PCI-DSS-compliant partners. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights
