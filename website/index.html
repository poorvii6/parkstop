Pricing

Transparent and fair

Drivers pay the hourly rate set by the space owner. ParkStop charges a small platform service fee on each booking — always shown before you confirm. Space owners receive their earnings, net of the platform fee, directly to their account.

Built on trust and security

Every payment on ParkStop is handled by a certified, RBI-regulated payment gateway with bank-grade encryption. We never store your card or bank details, and verified check-in protects both drivers and space owners on every booking.

Policies

Our policies

Transparency matters. Here's how we handle terms, privacy, and refunds.

Terms of Service

By using ParkStop, you agree to use the platform lawfully to find, book, or list parking spaces. ParkStop is a marketplace that connects drivers ("Finders") with parking-space owners ("Spotters"); the parking service itself is provided by the space owner. Bookings are subject to availability and the price shown at the time of booking. Users are responsible for the accuracy of the information they provide and for complying with local parking rules and regulations. ParkStop reserves the right to suspend accounts that misuse the platform.

Privacy Policy

We collect only the information needed to provide the service: your contact details, approximate location (to show nearby parking), booking history, and payment confirmation. Payments are processed by a certified third-party payment gateway — ParkStop does not store your card or bank details. We do not sell your personal data. Information is used to operate the service, process bookings, and provide support. You may request access to, or deletion of, your data by contacting us at the email below.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

  • A booking may be cancelled before check-in for a full refund.
  • After check-in, charges apply for the time the space was reserved or used.
  • If a booked space is unavailable on arrival, the driver receives a full refund.
  • Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method, typically within 5–7 business days.
  • For any billing issue, contact our support team and we will resolve it promptly.
