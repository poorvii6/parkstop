ParkStop — Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 5 August 2026

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains how cancellations and refunds work on the ParkStop platform. It forms part of our Terms of Service. All refunds are processed through our payment partner, Razorpay, back to the original payment method.

1. Cancellation by a Finder

If you are a Finder and cancel a confirmed booking, the refund depends on how far in advance you cancel, based on the booking’s start time:

When you cancelRefund
More than 1 hour before the booking start timeFull refund of the booking amount
Within 1 hour of the booking start time50% of the booking amount
After the booking start time / no-showNo refund

Note: these cancellation windows and amounts are set by ParkStop and may be adjusted; the version shown in the app at the time of your booking applies.

2. Cancellation by a Spotter

If a Spotter cancels a confirmed booking, or the parking space is unavailable or materially different from its listing, the Finder receives a full refund of the booking amount. Repeated cancellations by a Spotter may lead to penalties, reduced visibility, or removal from the Platform.

3. Platform Fee

The Platform Fee is generally non-refundable on Finder-initiated cancellations, except where the booking is cancelled by the Spotter, is not honoured, or fails due to an error on ParkStop’s side — in which case the full amount, including the Platform Fee, is refunded.

4. Failed or duplicate payments

If you are charged but your booking is not confirmed, or you are charged more than once for the same booking, the extra or failed amount is refunded in full. If money was debited but the booking did not confirm, it is typically auto-reversed by your bank within 5–7 business days; if not, contact us.

5. How and when refunds are paid

  • Approved refunds are initiated to your original payment method (UPI, card, or netbanking) via Razorpay.
  • Refunds usually reflect in your account within 5–7 business days after they are initiated, depending on your bank or UPI provider.
  • You will be notified in the app when a refund is initiated.

6. How to request a cancellation or refund

You can cancel an eligible booking directly in the ParkStop app. For refund issues, disputes, or questions, contact us at twin.win500@gmail.com with your booking details, and we will respond as soon as possible.

7. Disputes

Where a Finder and Spotter disagree about whether a booking was honoured, ParkStop may review the booking details and, at its reasonable discretion, decide the appropriate refund. Our decision in such cases is intended to be fair to both parties.
