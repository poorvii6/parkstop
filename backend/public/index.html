How It Works

Three steps to stress-free parking

01

Search Nearby Spots

Open ParkStop, see real-time available parking spots near you on a live map. Filter by price, distance, and vehicle type.

02

Book & Pay Securely

Reserve your spot with one tap. Pay securely through Razorpay — UPI, cards, or net banking. Receive an OTP to confirm arrival.

03

Park & Go

Show your OTP to the Spotter, park your vehicle, and drive away when done. Spotters earn on every completed booking.
